How to Play

Find the five-letter word

You get 8 guesses. Each guess must be a real five-letter word.

Read the two numbers

Exact means a letter is correct and in the right spot.

Present means a correct letter is in the wrong spot.

The challenge

Wordstake does not tell you which letters matched. Use the totals to narrow down the answer.

Mark your clues

Tap a submitted tile once for yellow, again for green, and once more for gray. These marks are your private notes.

Need help?

Use a hint to reveal one letter. Easy Mode can also show every matching tile. Turn it on in Settings.
