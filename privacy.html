Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Wordstake ("Wordstake," "we," "us") collects when you use the game at this website, why we collect it, and what control you have over it. Wordstake is operated by [OPERATOR NAME]. If you have questions, contact privacy@REPLACE_WITH_DOMAIN.

What we collect

You can play Wordstake without an account — in that case we don't collect any personal information, and your stats live only in your browser's memory for that session.

If you create an account, we collect:

  • Email address and password — used to authenticate you. Your password is never stored in plain text; authentication is handled by our backend provider (Supabase), which stores only a salted hash.
  • Username — the display name you choose, shown on the leaderboard.
  • Gameplay data — the words you guess, whether you won, how many guesses and hints you used, and the date you played, so we can show your stats, streak, and leaderboard position.

We do not collect payment information (Wordstake is free), and we do not run advertising trackers or analytics scripts on this site.

How we use it

  • To create and secure your account, and keep you signed in across visits.
  • To track your stats, streaks, and guess history, and to show them back to you.
  • To power the public leaderboard (your chosen username and streak count are visible to other players; your email is never shown to other players).
  • To maintain the fairness of the daily puzzle (e.g., preventing the same account from replaying a day's word on a second device).

Who we share it with

We don't sell your data. We use a small number of infrastructure providers to run the site, and your data passes through them as part of normal operation:

  • Supabase — hosts our database and handles authentication (account creation, sign-in, password storage).
  • Vercel — hosts and serves the website itself.
  • Google Fonts — the page loads one webfont from Google's servers, which sees your IP address like any web request, independent of your Wordstake account.

We may disclose information if required by law, or to protect the security or integrity of the service.

Cookies and local storage

Wordstake uses your browser's local storage (not tracking cookies) to keep you signed in between visits, via our authentication provider's standard session-handling. We don't use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

Data retention

We keep your account and gameplay history for as long as your account exists, so your streak and stats stay intact. If you'd like your account and associated data deleted, email privacy@REPLACE_WITH_DOMAIN — account deletion is currently handled manually on request rather than through a self-serve button in the app.

Children's privacy

Wordstake is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we'll remove it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data. To exercise any of these, email privacy@REPLACE_WITH_DOMAIN.
